# Notchy v1.6.0

## Menu bar, not the notch

- Notchy now lives in the menu bar as a real `NSStatusItem`, alongside Wi-Fi, battery and the clock.
- The notch window layer is gone — no overlay parked over the notch, and no bounding box to click around.
- Works on any Mac running macOS 14+, not just models with a physical notch.

## The icon carries the status

- The active agent's mark tints itself: green = working, yellow = waiting on you, red = error.
- Idle renders as a template image, so it goes monochrome and matches every other menu bar icon in light and dark.
- No separate coloured dot left glowing next to the camera indicator.

## Click, don't hover

- Click the icon to open the status panel; click again or click away to dismiss.
- Hover-to-expand is gone, along with the stray redraws it caused.

## Under the hood

- Status and usage for all three agents now build from a single snapshot list, with the most recently active agent driving the icon.
- Noticeably less lag than the notch-window approach.

## Install

Download `Notchy.pkg`, open it, approve it once in Privacy & Security if prompted, and restart active agent sessions so their hooks reload.
